About our solution

Safety should never be left to chance.

Gas leakage can put people, property and businesses at risk. Ignis Secure is a proposed IoT-based gas safety system that detects LPG leaks and provides real-time alerts, helping users become aware of a potential leak and respond promptly.

Our goal is to make gas safety monitoring simpler, smarter and more accessible for both household and industrial settings.
